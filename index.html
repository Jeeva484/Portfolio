Peer-Reviewed Research Publication

Published at IEEE ICGCCT 2026

Presented & Published: March 2026

“Hemorrhagic Stroke Prediction Using Deep Learning Algorithm”

Jeeva Sugin D | IEEE International Conference on Green Computing for Communication Technologies (ICGCCT 2026)

Abstract & Clinical Significance: Early identification of intracranial hemorrhagic lesions from non-contrast computed tomography (CT) scans is critical to minimize permanent neurological deficit and mortality. This research proposes an optimized deep Convolutional Neural Network architecture coupled with automated OpenCV preprocessing pipelines (contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalization and automated skull stripping). The resulting pipeline demonstrates high clinical sensitivity and low inference latency suitable for real-time emergency room diagnostic support.
